罗茸，中国人民大学经济学院副教授、经济学院企业经济教研室主任、社会科学高等研究院（深圳）数字经济研究中心副主任，宾夕法尼亚州立大学经济学博士。

研究方向是产业组织经济学，现有研究主题涉及双边市场网络效应、消费者离散选择需求模型、企业动态定价、企业上下游合约、企业生产率估计，以及独家合约对二手房价的影响。研究成果发表在 Management Science、International Economic Review、Journal of Development Economics、International Journal of Industrial Organization、Journal of Economics & Management Strategy 等期刊，主持国家自然科学基金青年项目和面上项目，担任 China Economic Review 期刊副编辑。

Rong Luo is an Associate Professor at the School of Economics, Renmin University of China. She also serves as Director of the Department of Business Economics and Deputy Director of the Digital Economy Research Center at the Advanced Institute of Social Sciences (Shenzhen). She received her Ph.D. in Economics from The Pennsylvania State University.

Her research fields are industrial organization and applied microeconomics. Her current work examines network effects in two-sided markets, discrete-choice demand, firms' dynamic pricing, vertical contracts, productivity estimation, and the effects of exclusive contracts on second-hand housing prices. Her research has appeared in Management Science, International Economic Review, Journal of Development Economics, International Journal of Industrial Organization, and Journal of Economics & Management Strategy. She has led National Natural Science Foundation of China projects and serves as an Associate Editor of China Economic Review.
